Simple Shooter: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Simple Shooter ("the game"), published by gamez85, handles and why.

Information the game keeps on your device

The game saves your best score, your personal top 10 and your sound setting on your own device so they are there next time you play. This information never leaves your device through us: we do not run servers for the game and do not collect, receive or sell it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To deliver, measure and protect ads, Google may collect and process information from your device, including:

  • device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • information about your device and the game, and about your interactions with ads (for example views and taps).

How Google uses this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time from Privacy options on the game's title screen. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's settings under Google > Ads (the exact menu depends on your device).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
