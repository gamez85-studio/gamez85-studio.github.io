Dead Letters: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy explains what information the Android app Dead Letters ("the app"), a spirit board magic trick published by gamez85, handles and why.

What you type stays on the screen

The invocation, the secret answer and the question you type are only used to play the trick on screen. They are kept in the phone's memory while the session lasts and are never saved, sent anywhere or seen by us.

Information the app keeps on your device

The app saves your settings on your own device so they are there next time: the spirit's name, the invocation you chose, whether the final scare, vibration and sound are on, the board you use and until when any extra board is unlocked, and your language. This information never leaves your device through us: we do not run servers for the app and do not collect, receive or sell it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC: a banner on the menu, a full-screen ad now and then between tricks, and an optional video that unlocks a board for a day. To deliver, measure and protect ads, Google may collect and process information from your device, including:

  • device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • information about your device and the app, and about your interactions with ads (for example views and taps).

How Google uses this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time from Privacy options in the app's Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's settings under Google > Ads (the exact menu depends on your device).

Children

The app contains a sudden scare and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
