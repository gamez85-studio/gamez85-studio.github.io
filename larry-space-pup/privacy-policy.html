Larry Space Pup: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Larry Space Pup ("the game"), published by gamez85, handles and why. The game is made for players of all ages, including children, and is designed to follow Google Play's Families policy and the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

We do not collect personal information

The game has no accounts, no chat and no way to talk to other players. We do not run servers for the game, and we do not collect, receive, store or sell any personal information from anyone who plays it, children included.

Information the game keeps on your device

The game saves your progress on your own device so it is there next time you play: the story, side quests, golden bones, time trial medals, spore coins, the looks you have bought with them, your sticker album, the days you have played, and your sound and language settings. This information never leaves your device through us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Photos

In photo mode the game can make a picture of the game screen (only the game: never the camera). A photo stays on your device unless the player chooses to share it with the device's own share menu; the game does not upload it anywhere.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC, set up for an audience that includes children:

  • every ad request is marked as directed to children and to users under the age of consent, so Google treats it accordingly (no personalised or interest-based ads, no remarketing);
  • ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences (rated G);
  • the game does not use the Android advertising ID or the Privacy Sandbox ad services;
  • full-screen ads appear only now and then at natural pauses, and a video ad plays only when the player taps "×2 VIDEO" to double a reward. Watching it is never needed to play.

To deliver ads and to protect them against fraud, Google may still process limited technical information, such as the device's IP address and the type of device. How Google handles information is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in Google's information on child-directed treatment.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), Google's consent tools may ask about ads before any are shown.

Links

The only link out of the game is the one to this policy, on the title screen.

Parents

If you have any question about your child's use of the game, or believe information about your child has reached us by mistake, write to us and we will help, and delete it if it exists.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
